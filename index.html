About

I've spent the last four years on the ingestion side of the data stack — batch and near-real-time ETL in Python, PySpark and Kafka, landing in Delta Lake and Snowflake, modeled with dbt and orchestrated with Airflow. At Digit Insurance that meant pulling claims, policy, billing and API sources into one lakehouse; at SDSU it means turning telemetry, mission logs, APIs and geospatial data into something research teams can query without asking me first.

The part I care most about is data quality — Great Expectations suites, dbt tests and OpenLineage tracking so that schema drift surfaces at ingest, not three dashboards later. Outside work I build the application layer too: Next.js and FastAPI services with real scoring engines, retrieval pipelines and payment flows behind them.

I hold an M.S. in Big Data Analytics from San Diego State, and I'm looking for data engineer, data analyst and software engineer roles.
